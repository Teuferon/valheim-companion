Privacy Policy

Fan project, zero tracking without your permission, and no advertisements.

Fan Project & No Accounts

Valheim Companion is a free, fan-made tool created in spare time. There are no user accounts, no logins, and no advertisements. You can use all tools freely without registering.

Local Browser Storage

Your settings and preferences remain on your device. We use browser localStorage solely to save your choices across visits:

  • vc.language / vc.player — Preferences such as language, theme, and tool configurations
  • vc.progress — Your checklist progress across biomes and bosses
  • vc.consent — Your cookie and analytics consent preference

None of this data contains personal information or ever leaves your browser.

Google Analytics 4 & Consent Mode v2

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-CXQVNCCJKE) to see how players use the companion and which features are most helpful. We respect your privacy using Google Consent Mode v2:

  • Default state: all analytics and advertising storage is denied. Google receives only anonymous, cookieless pings.
  • Consent granted: analytics cookies are enabled only if you click Allow in the cookie banner.
  • Production only: analytics scripts are never loaded on localhost, development, or preview servers.

No Advertising & No Data Selling

We do not display ads, do not use ad tracking tags, and will never sell or monetize your data. Advertising and personalization signals remain permanently denied.
